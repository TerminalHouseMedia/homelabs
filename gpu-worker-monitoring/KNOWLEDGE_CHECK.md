# Knowledge checks

Practice questions; these do not assert completed operator assessment.

1. Which metrics originate in windows_exporter versus nvidia-smi?
2. Why convert reported MiB to bytes?
3. Why use an invariant numeric culture?
4. Why replace a temporary textfile atomically?
5. How do collection success and timestamp age differ?
6. Why can a scrape succeed while GPU data is stale?
7. What does an unavailable sleeping worker look like?
8. Why restrict the exporter firewall rule to Prometheus?
9. Why avoid overlapping task execution?
10. Which features are deliberately outside this monitoring project?

## Practical tasks

1. Explain the architecture and trace one measurement or operation through every layer without reading the commands.
2. In a disposable environment, execute the README’s recovery practice, capture before/after evidence, and explain the rollback limits.
