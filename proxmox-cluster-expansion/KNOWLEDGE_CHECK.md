# Knowledge checks

Practice questions; these do not assert completed operator assessment.

1. What does quorum protect?
2. What does Corosync provide?
3. Why is two-node membership different from HA?
4. Which join prerequisites protect existing guests?
5. Why must hostnames and addressing be stable?
6. Why align versions before joining?
7. Does a cluster make local storage shared?
8. Where should a QDevice be placed and why?
9. Which read-only commands prove membership and storage visibility?
10. Why is node removal a separate recovery-sensitive procedure?

## Practical tasks

1. Explain the architecture and trace one measurement or operation through every layer without reading the commands.
2. In a disposable environment, execute the README’s recovery practice, capture before/after evidence, and explain the rollback limits.
